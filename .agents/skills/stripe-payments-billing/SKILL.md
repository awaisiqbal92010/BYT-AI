# Stripe Payments & Billing Skill

Use this skill when implementing monetization, pricing tables, monthly/annual toggles, and payment workflows.

## Billing Patterns

1. **Pricing Cards Architecture**
   - Provide Monthly vs Annual billing toggle with calculated discount badges (e.g. `Save 20%`).
   - Highlight the "Most Popular" plan with distinct border glow, badge, and primary CTA button.

2. **Feature Comparison Matrices**
   - Use Lucide `<CheckCircle2 className="text-emerald-500" />` for included features and muted dashes for excluded features.
