# Typographic Scale & Visual Hierarchy

## Principles
- **Major Second / Minor Third Ratio**: 12px, 14px, 16px, 20px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 64px.
- **Optical Leading**: Tighter line-height for large display headings (`leading-tight`); relaxed for body (`leading-relaxed`).
- **Tabular Figures**: Always use `font-mono tabular-nums` for counters, currency, and timestamps to prevent jitter.
- **Letter Spacing**: Use `tracking-tight` on headings >24px, neutral tracking on body, `tracking-wider` on uppercase tags.
